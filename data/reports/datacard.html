Relevance judgments and splits

Up to 40 human-judged candidates per query. Gains follow the ESCI paper (E 1.0, S 0.1, C 0.01, I 0). Relevance outside a query's judged set is unknown, so full-catalog retrieval metrics report judged@k instead of treating unjudged products as irrelevant.

Simulated behaviour

Traffic, clicks and conversions

A deliberately imperfect logging ranker with 5% randomised exploration, a position-based click model and a cart → purchase funnel, all grounded in the real labels. Useful for learning from feedback, caching and load tests, and for keeping offline relevance separate from conversion. It is not evidence of real conversion impact.

Replay stream for caching and latency

A 24-hour request stream. A share of requests are one-off variants of a known query, so cache hit rates stay realistic; each keeps its parent query id, which makes the variants a robustness slice too.
